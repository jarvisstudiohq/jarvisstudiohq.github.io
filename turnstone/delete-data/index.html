Turnstone

Delete your Turnstone data

  • Page version 1
  • Effective October 27, 2026

Turnstone can erase everything it keeps about you, from inside the game, at any time. You do not need to contact us, and there is no account to close.

This version of Turnstone keeps all of its data on your device and sends none of it to us (see section 6 of the Privacy Policy, version 4, effective October 27, 2026). That is why deleting happens on the device.

How to delete it in the game

  1. Open Settings (from the menu on Home, or from the Pause menu during a level).
  2. In the Account and social section, tap Delete my data (“Erase everything Turnstone keeps about you.”).
  3. The game lists what will be erased from the device:
    • Your progress: levels, stars, Daily and Tide results
    • Shells, Tools, Tide Jars and cosmetics
    • Your name, age group and Terms acceptance
    • Settings, quests, achievements and stats
    • Level drafts and boards you made on this device
    It also says: “This version of Turnstone has no online account. Everything it keeps is on this device, so nothing is stored on a server.” and “This cannot be undone. Afterwards Turnstone starts again from the first level.” Tap Delete my data to go on, or Keep my data to leave.
  4. The game asks “Are you sure?” Tap Yes, delete everything, or Cancel.
  5. The game shows “Turnstone has erased its data from this device.” and “There was no online account to delete.”
    • On Android, tap Close Turnstone.
    • On iPhone and iPad, close it yourself: swipe up from the bottom edge and swipe Turnstone away.
  6. The next time you open Turnstone, it starts fresh, as if it were newly installed: it asks your age, shows the Terms, and begins at the first level.

If something could not be erased

If the game says “Some files could not be erased.”, tap Try again. If it keeps failing, uninstalling Turnstone removes them.

Other ways your data is removed

  • Uninstalling the game also removes its data from your device.
  • Your device’s own backups (for example, a phone backup you made) may keep a copy until they are replaced.
  • Because this version has no account and stores nothing with us, there is nothing on our side to delete.

For parents and guardians

You can erase everything the game keeps by using “Delete my data” in Settings on your child’s device.

Questions

Email jarvisstudiohq@gmail.com. If you have written to us and want that email erased, write to the same address. When a later version adds an online account, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how deleting the account works.
