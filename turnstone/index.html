About the game

Turnstone is a cozy tile puzzle set in a sunny harbor town, starring Otto, a round purple octopus who’s never in a hurry and hopes you won’t be either. It’s turn-based and made for one thumb: tap a tile to flip the path between two matching Twins, and three or more matching tiles clear. Take your time. There are no timers, no lives and no energy, so nothing ever makes you wait to keep playing.

Play the campaign through the harbor, try the Daily puzzle that’s the same for everyone, and build your own levels in the level maker.

Good to know

  • One thumb, taps onlyPortrait and turn-based. No timer runs while you think.
  • No lives, no energyYou can always play another level. Nothing makes you wait.
  • Every tile has a symbolStars, leaves, shells and sails, so color is never the only clue.
  • A fair DailyThe same puzzle for everyone, played without Tools. Stars are never sold.
  • Plays offlineThis version keeps your progress on your device and needs no account.
  • Free to playSupported by ads and optional in-app purchases, which aren’t switched on yet. Never in the ranked Daily.
