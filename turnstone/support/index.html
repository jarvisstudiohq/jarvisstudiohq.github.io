Questions

How do I delete my data?

In the game: Settings › Account and social › Delete my data, then confirm with Yes, delete everything. Turnstone erases everything it keeps on your device and starts fresh the next time you open it. The full steps are on Delete your data.

How do I contact you?

Email jarvisstudiohq@gmail.com. You can also find the address inside the game: Settings › About › Contact us.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. This version works offline and has no account. On Android it does not even ask for internet access.

I got a new phone, or lost my progress. Can you restore it?

This version keeps your progress only on your device and never sends it to us, so we have no copy to restore and there is no cloud save yet. A backup of your device that you made yourself may still hold a copy.

Are there ads or purchases?

This version of Turnstone does not sell anything for real money and does not show advertising. If we ever add purchases or ads, the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy will be updated first and the game will ask you to accept them again.

Why does the game ask my age?

Once, the first time you open it, so it can set itself up for you. For example, players under 13 pick their name from a list instead of typing one. It keeps only an age group, never your birthday or exact age. See section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

Where can I get Turnstone?

Turnstone is coming to iOS and Android. It is not in the stores yet.
