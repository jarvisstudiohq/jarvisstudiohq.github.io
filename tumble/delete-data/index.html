Tumble

Delete your Tumble data

  • Page version 1
  • Effective October 27, 2026

Tumble can erase everything it keeps about you, from inside the game, at any time. You don't need to contact us, and there's no account to close.

This version of Tumble keeps all of its data on your device and sends none of it to us (see section 7 of the Privacy Policy, version 4, effective October 29, 2026). That’s why deleting happens on the device.

How to delete it in the game

  1. Open Settings from the Home screen.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete my data (“Erase everything Tumble keeps about you.”).
  3. The game lists what will be erased from the device:
    • Your age group and Terms acceptance
    • Your settings
    • Your saved run, your run history, your tutorial progress and your Daily Drop record
    • Your coins and the looks you own
    • Your ads and purchases file (Remove ads, ad counters)
    It also says “This cannot be undone. Afterwards Tumble starts again as if it was just installed.” Tap Delete my data to go on, or Keep my data to leave.
  4. The game asks “Are you sure?” Tap Yes, delete everything, or Cancel.
  5. The game shows “Tumble has erased its data from this device.” and “There was no online account to delete.”
    • On Android, tap Close Tumble.
    • On iPhone and iPad, close it yourself: swipe up from the bottom edge and swipe Tumble away.
  6. The next time you open Tumble, it starts fresh, as if it were newly installed.

If something could not be erased

If the game says “Some files could not be erased.”, tap Try again. If it keeps failing, uninstalling Tumble removes them.

Other ways your data is removed

  • Uninstalling the game also removes its data from your device.
  • Your device’s own backups (for example, a phone backup you made) may keep a copy until they’re replaced.
  • Deleting your data doesn’t undo a purchase. Apple or Google keep their own record of what you bought, and Restore purchases can give you Remove ads again. It can’t bring back coins you’ve already spent or lost.
  • Because this version has no account and stores nothing with us, there’s nothing on our side to delete.

For parents and guardians

You can erase everything the game keeps by using “Delete my data” in Settings on your child’s device.

Questions

Email jarvisstudiohq@gmail.com. If you’ve written to us and want that email erased, write to the same address. When a later version adds an online account, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how deleting the account works.
