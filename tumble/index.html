About the game

Tumble is a drop-and-fuse block puzzle for one thumb, played in portrait. Block pieces drop into a well. When two pieces of the same size touch, they fuse into a bigger one, and fused pieces can chain. Full rows clear, and two of the biggest pieces make a Tumble.

A rising floor slowly pushes the rows up, but a meter and a preview always show it coming, so it’s never a surprise. Each size of piece is a tier, so the bigger the piece, the higher the tier.

Classic is endless: play until the well fills. The Daily Drop is the same puzzle for everyone, each day. Drag to aim, let go to drop, and collect bright candy looks for your blocks along the way.
