About the studio

Jarvis Studios is a small, independent studio that makes hand-made games for phones: warm, readable and played with one thumb.

What we believe

A good phone game should fit around your day, not the other way around. So our games are built to be read before they’re played: nothing is hidden, and every choice is yours to size up before you make it.

  • Kind to your time. No lives, no energy, and no timers that make you wait or hurry.
  • Fair. Nothing you can buy wins for you, and every ranked Daily is the same for everyone.
  • Honest about money. Our games are free to play, supported by ads and optional in-app purchases. They aren’t switched on yet. When they are, ads are optional or limited to a few moments between runs, and never in a ranked Daily.
  • Readable. Color is never the only clue, and text sizes can grow.
  • Private. We collect as little as we can, and we say exactly what, in plain words.

Our games

  • Coming to iOS and Android

    Turnstone

    A cozy, turn-based tile puzzle in a sunny harbor, played with one thumb. No timers, no lives and no energy.

  • In development

    Tumble

    A bright drop-and-fuse block puzzle for one thumb. Fuse matching pieces into bigger ones, chain them and clear the rows, in Classic or the Daily Drop.

And the cat?

A ginger cat naps on the quay in Turnstone’s harbor. She’s become the studio’s mascot, and she turns up wherever there’s a warm spot to sleep. Look for her around this site, and in our games.
