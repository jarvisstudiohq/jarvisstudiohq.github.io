Meet Turnstone

October 27, 2026 · Jarvis Studios

Turnstone is our first game: a cozy tile puzzle set in a sunny harbor town, starring Otto, a round purple octopus who’s never in a hurry and hopes you won’t be either.

Every level is a board of glazed tiles marked with stars, leaves, shells and sails. Tap a tile to flip the path between two matching Twins, and three or more matching tiles clear. It’s turn-based and made for one thumb, so it plays just as well on the bus, in line or in bed.

What we promise

  • No timers that rush you, and no lives or energy that make you wait.
  • Every tile carries a symbol, so color is never the only clue.
  • Nothing you can buy is ever usable in the ranked Daily puzzle, and stars are never sold.

Turnstone will be free to play, supported by ads and optional in-app purchases. Neither is switched on in this version.

Turnstone is coming to iOS and Android. It isn’t in the stores yet. When it is, the store buttons on this site will start working.
