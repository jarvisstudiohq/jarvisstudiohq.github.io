Fair by design: no lives, no energy

October 27, 2026 · Jarvis Studios

We want Turnstone to be a game you can trust with your evening.

So it has no lives and no energy: you can always play another level, and you never have to wait or pay to keep going. There are no timers either. Every move waits for you.

The Daily puzzle is the same board for everyone, and it is played without Tools, so nothing you can buy helps you on it. Stars, the record of how well you played a level, are never for sale.

This version of Turnstone shows no ads and sells nothing for real money. If that ever changes, the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy will be updated first, and the game will ask you to accept them again.
