Turnstone

How do I delete my data?

In the game: Settings › Account and social › Delete my data, then confirm with Yes, delete everything. Turnstone erases everything it keeps on your device and starts fresh the next time you open it. The full steps are on Delete your data.

How do I contact you?

Email jarvisstudiohq@gmail.com. You can also find the address inside the game: Settings › About › Contact us.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. This version works offline and has no account. On Android it doesn’t even ask for internet access.

I got a new phone, or lost my progress. Can you restore it?

This version keeps your progress only on your device and never sends it to us, so we have no copy to restore, and there’s no cloud save yet. A backup you made of your device may still have one.

Are there ads or purchases?

Turnstone will be free to play, supported by ads and optional in-app purchases. This version shows no ads and sells nothing for real money. When they arrive, ads will be optional rewarded ads, they will never appear in the ranked Daily, and a Remove ads purchase will be available. Players under 13 will see no ads at all, and no one under 18 will see personalized ads. The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy will be updated first, and the game will ask you to accept them again.

Why does the game ask my age?

Just once, the first time you open it, so it can set itself up for you. For example, players under 13 pick their name from a list instead of typing one. It keeps only an age group, never your birthday or exact age. See section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

Where can I get Turnstone?

Turnstone is coming to iOS and Android. It isn’t in the stores yet.

Tumble

How do I play?

Drag across the well to aim the piece, then let go to drop it. The Rotate button turns it, Hold sets one aside for later, and the Drop button works too. When two pieces of the same size touch, they fuse into a bigger one. Full rows clear, and two of the biggest pieces make a Tumble. You can replay the lessons any time from How to play.

What is the Daily Drop?

The same puzzle for everyone, each day. Nothing you can buy is usable in it, and ads never appear in it. For now, the online leaderboard isn’t open, so the game shows only your own score, kept on your device.

What are coins and looks?

Looks change how Tumble looks and sounds: blocks, boards, effects and more. Coins buy looks only, never a win. Coins have no cash value and can’t be sold or traded.

Are there ads? What do Remove ads and Restore purchases do?

Tumble will be free to play, supported by ads and optional in-app purchases. Ads, coin packs and Remove ads aren’t switched on yet. When they are, ads will be optional rewarded ads (such as “Keep going” or free coins). We may later add an occasional ad between runs in Classic, which Remove ads would remove. Ads never appear in the Daily Drop. Players under 13, and anyone whose age group isn’t known, aren’t offered rewarded ads and never see personalized ads.

Remove ads takes away the ads you didn’t choose, and the optional ones stay yours to pick. If you bought it before, Restore purchases in Settings gets it back on this device. It can’t bring back coins you’ve already spent or lost.

How do I delete my data?

In the game: Settings › Account › Delete my data, then confirm with Yes, delete everything. Tumble erases everything it keeps on your device and starts fresh the next time you open it. The full steps are on Delete your data.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. This version works offline and has no account. On Android it doesn’t even ask for internet access.

How do I contact you?

Email jarvisstudiohq@gmail.com. You can also find the address inside the game, under Settings › About.

Where can I get Tumble?

Tumble is coming soon to iOS and Android. It isn’t in the stores yet.
