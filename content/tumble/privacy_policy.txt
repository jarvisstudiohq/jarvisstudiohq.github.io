# The short version

This version of Tumble works offline. It keeps your game data on your device and does not send it to us or to anyone else. It has no account, no player name, no ads, no purchases and no analytics or crash reporting service. You can erase everything it keeps from Settings, under "Delete my data". Optional ads and purchases are planned; before we turn on any feature that sends information off your device, we will update this policy and ask you to accept it again.

# 1. Who we are

Tumble is made by Jarvis Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game keeps, where it keeps it, and what you can do about it.

# 2. What this version keeps, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the game's own storage. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.

• Your age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and up), and the calendar year when it can next move up. The game turns the age you choose into a group and then forgets the number. It never stores your birthday or your exact age, and never shows your age group to anyone.

• Which version of the Terms of Use and of this policy you accepted, and when.

• Your game progress: your last 20 finished runs (for each, its seed and run code, mode, date, score, number of drops, best tier, best chain and how it ended), your best scores, your Daily Drop result for each recent day, and totals such as how many runs you have played. A run you have not finished is saved too, so you can pick it up again.

• Your coins (your balance, and totals of what you have earned and spent), which runs and Daily Drops have already paid out, and the cosmetic looks you own and have equipped.

• Your settings: music and sound effects, haptics, reduced motion, text size and the way you aim.

• A small file about ads and purchases: whether Remove ads is yours and where it came from, short codes made from store receipts (never the receipts themselves), and counters for the optional ads, such as how many you have watched today and when.

• Backup copies of these files, so a save is not lost if something goes wrong.

While the game is running, it also keeps a short list of recent ad and purchase events in memory (for example, "an ad was offered"). The list holds no age, name or device ID. It is never saved and never sent, and it is gone when you close the game.

The Daily Drop leaderboard is not online. The game shows only your own score, and nothing is posted anywhere.

We use this information only to run the game for you: to remember your progress, to set up the game for your age group, and to keep your saves safe.

# 3. What this version does not do

This version of Tumble does not:

• send your information to us or to anyone else (apart from an email you choose to write to us, see section 12; on Android it does not even ask for internet access);

• create an account, or ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location;

• read your device's advertising ID or hardware IDs;

• show ads or take payments, or track you across other apps or websites;

• use an analytics, crash reporting, advertising or social service from another company;

• sell or share your personal information;

• let you talk with other players, or see them.

The game uses your device's vibration only for haptics, which you can turn off in Settings. It also reads your device's language and country setting on the device, only to decide whether to show a money amount next to coin prices and, later, which ad consent question applies. It does not store or send that setting.

# 4. Information Apple and Google may hold

You download Tumble from Apple's App Store or Google Play. They handle your download and your store account under their own privacy policies. If you have allowed your device to share diagnostics or usage data with app developers, Apple or Google may give us crash reports and statistics about the game. We use those only to find and fix problems.

# 5. Children and teens

People of all ages can play Tumble. The first time you open the game, it asks for your age on a slider. Nothing is picked for you, the question does not say what any answer unlocks, and it is asked only once. The game keeps only your age group, as described in section 2. Your group moves up by itself as the years pass. In Settings you can move to a younger group at any time, but you cannot move to an older one.

What each age group means:

• Under 13, and a player whose age group is not known: no ads, ever. They are never offered "Keep going" or the free coins ad, and the game never asks an ad company for one.

• 13 to 17: only non-personalized ads, and only the optional ones, if ads are on.

• 18 and up: ads may be personalized, but only where the law allows it and you have said yes in the consent question.

In this version nothing is sent off the device for any age group, so the game gives us no information about any child. A parent or guardian can erase everything the game keeps by using "Delete my data" in Settings on the child's device. The game does not check who is making a purchase: Apple's and Google's parental controls do that.

# 6. Ads and purchases when they are on

Ads and purchases are not on in this version. This section says what will happen when they are, so you know now. Before they are turned on in a release, we will update this policy with the exact ad company and store, and ask you to accept it again.

Optional ads. Tumble's ads are ones you choose to watch: "Keep going" after a Classic run, and free coins on Home. Ads between runs are switched off; if we ever turn them on, we will update this policy first. The Daily Drop, onboarding and the middle of a run never show an ad. When ads are enabled, an ad network will provide them. To show an ad, the network's software will run inside the game and may use your device's advertising ID, your IP address, your device model and settings, and what you do with the ad (for example, that it finished). The network receives this from your device directly; we do not collect it. We tell the network your age group so that it does not show a child personalized ads (it is told to treat players under 13 and players of unknown age as children, and to give 13- to 17-year-olds only non-personalized ads). In places where the law requires it, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and where the place is not known, the game will show a consent question before any ad, and a way to change your answer in Settings. If you refuse, no ad is shown, and you lose nothing else.

Purchases. Coin packs and Remove ads are paid for through Apple or Google. They take the payment and give us a receipt for the purchase. We never see your card or payment details. The game keeps only what it needs to give you what you bought: your coin balance, whether you own Remove ads, and short codes made from receipts so that one purchase is never counted twice. "Restore purchases" asks the store what your store account owns.

Region. If you are in the European Union or the European Economic Area, the game shows an approximate money amount next to a coin price. It picks the region from your store country, or, if the store gives none, your device's country setting. It does not keep it.

The game also keeps its ad and purchase events only in memory, as section 2 says. They are not an analytics service.

# 7. Keeping and deleting your information

The game keeps your information on your device until you delete it. To erase all of it: open Settings, go to Account, choose "Delete my data", and confirm. Tumble then erases everything it keeps on the device: your age group and Terms acceptance, your settings, your runs and Daily Drop results, your coins and looks, and your ads and purchases file. The next time you open it, it starts fresh, as if it were newly installed.

Uninstalling the game also removes its data from your device. Your device's own backups (for example, a phone backup you made) may keep a copy until they are replaced.

Deleting your data does not undo a purchase. Apple or Google keep their own record of what you bought; "Restore purchases" can give you Remove ads again, but not coins you have already spent or lost.

Because this version has no account and the game stores nothing with us, there is nothing on our side to delete, other than any email you chose to send us (see section 12).

# 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as the right to see it, get a copy, correct it or delete it. In this version, all of it is on your device: you can see most of it in the game and delete all of it with "Delete my data". For a question or request about your information, you can also write to us (section 12). We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

# 9. Security

The game writes its saves carefully so they are not lost or damaged, and keeps them in app storage that other apps cannot normally read. Your device's own security, such as its screen lock, protects them too. A save file is not proof of a purchase: the store's own record is.

# 10. When online features arrive

Later versions may add features such as an online account and cloud save, a Daily Drop leaderboard, purchases, optional ads, analytics or crash reports. Each of these would send some information off your device. Before any of them is turned on, we will:

• update this policy to say exactly what is collected, why, who receives it and how long it is kept;

• show you the new version in the game and ask you to accept it again;

• ask for your consent, or a parent's consent, where the law requires it;

• keep ads off for players under 13, and never show personalized ads to players under 18.

# 11. Changes to this policy

The version number and effective date are shown at the top. If we change this policy in an important way, the game will show you the new version and ask you to accept it before you continue.

# 12. Contact

You can read this policy at any time in Settings, under About. For questions, problems, privacy requests and reports, write to us at jarvisstudiohq@gmail.com. In Settings, under About, "Contact us" shows this address and lets you copy it or open your email app. The game itself sends us nothing. If you write to us, that is your own choice: your email program sends the message from your own account, and we receive your email address and whatever you choose to put in it. We use that only to answer you and to deal with your request, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising. If you write to us and later want that email erased, ask us at the same address. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to write for you, and please do not send us personal details you do not need to.
