Optional ads, coin packs and Remove ads are planned; they are not on yet, and you never have to watch an ad or pay to keep playing. The game keeps your progress on your device. You can delete your data at any time from Settings.

# 7. Money and advertising

Ads, coin packs and Remove ads are not on yet. This version of Tumble does not show ads and does not sell anything. We will turn them on only in a later release, after we update these Terms and the Privacy Policy and ask you to accept them again. If you are using a test copy of the game, any prices or ads you see there are pretend: nothing is charged, and no advertising company is involved.

When they are on, this is how they work.

Optional ads. Tumble's ads are ones you choose to watch (rewarded ads):

• "Keep going" in a Classic run: after a run ends, you may watch a short ad to take back your last drop and carry on, once per run.

• Free coins on Home: you may watch a short ad for a small number of coins, with a limit each day and a short wait between ads.

Saying no costs you nothing. The Daily Drop, onboarding and the middle of a run never show an ad. Ads that appear without your choosing them (such as one between runs) are switched off. If we ever turn them on, they will be limited, never shown in the Daily Drop, and Remove ads will remove them. When ads are on, an advertising company will provide them. It follows the age rules in section 2 and our Privacy Policy, and where the law requires it, asks for your consent first. If an ad cannot play, nothing is lost and nothing is granted.

Coin packs. Coin packs are virtual currency, bought with real money through Apple or Google at the price they show you. The price in your own currency and the payment are handled by the store, under the store's terms. Your first coin pack also gives a one-time bonus of extra coins. In some places, such as the European Union and the European Economic Area, the game also shows an approximate money amount next to a coin price. Coins have no cash value (section 6). Purchases are made through the store, so refunds, cancelations and complaints go to the store under its policy; where the law gives you a right to a refund or to cancel, you keep it.

Remove ads. Remove ads is a one-time purchase that removes ads you did not choose. It does not remove optional ads such as Keep going and free coins: those stay yours to pick. "Restore purchases" in Settings asks the store what your store account owns and gives you Remove ads again, for example on a new phone. If the store tells us a purchase was refunded, Remove ads may stop.

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before buying anything. The game does not check who is paying: the store's own controls do.

These rules are part of how the game is built, and they will not change:

• There are no lives and no energy. You never have to wait, or pay, to keep playing.

• Money never buys a win, a score or a rank.

• Nothing paid can be used in the Daily Drop, and the Daily Drop has no ads.
