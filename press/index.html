Tumble fact sheet

Game
Tumble, a portrait drop-and-fuse block puzzle
Status
In development. Coming soon to iOS and Android, with no release date yet.
How it works
Block pieces drop into a well. Two pieces of the same size fuse into a bigger one (size is the tier), and fused pieces can chain. Full rows clear, and two of the biggest make a Tumble.
Rising floor
A floor slowly pushes the rows up, with a meter and a preview so it’s never a surprise.
Modes
Classic, which is endless, and the Daily Drop, the same puzzle for everyone each day
Controls
One thumb: drag to aim, let go to drop
Look
Bright candy blocks, with looks to collect
Price
Free to play, supported by ads and optional in-app purchases (optional rewarded ads, possibly an occasional ad between runs in Classic later, and a Remove ads purchase; never in the Daily Drop)
